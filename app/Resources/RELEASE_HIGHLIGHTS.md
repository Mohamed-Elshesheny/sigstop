**The app now opens on machines that are not mine.**

Every build before v0.1.5 resolved its message corpus through the build directory of the
computer that compiled it, so it died before showing anything: no window, no Dock icon,
nothing in the menu bar, and no error to report. If you downloaded sigstop and it seemed to
do nothing at all, that was this.

The menu bar icon also stops claiming a position ahead of icons you arranged yourself. It
appears where new items go now, and Command-drag moves it anywhere you like.
